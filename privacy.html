Privacy policy

Effective: October 13, 2026 · Applies to the Ludo Buddy game for Android (package buddy.ludo.game), including Ludo and Snakes & Ladders, offline and online.

The short version. Offline games stay on your phone. If you play online, we store the name you choose, your moves and quick-chat emotes so the match works, and delete them 24 hours later. We use Google Firebase Analytics to see how the game is used, Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix crashes, and Google AdMob to show ads on the menus and after each finished match (never during play). Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK) we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Home → Privacy. We never ask for your real name, email, phone number, contacts or precise location, and we never sell your data.

1. Who we are

Ludo Buddy is developed by LunarSprint ("we", "us"). Contact us about anything in this policy at lunarsprintapps@gmail.com.

This is stored only on your device, never sent to us, and deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

4. Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played, so we can improve it.

Events we log

  • Which screens are opened.
  • Matches started, finished or quit: which game (Ludo or Snakes & Ladders), the mode (vs computer, pass & play or online), the number of players and computer players, how long the match lasted, and whether you won.
  • Online actions (quick match, create room, join room), which emote was sent, the board style you picked, and taps on Share and Rate.
  • Shop and rewards: opening the shop, starting or completing the "Remove ads" purchase (product name only), which board you unlocked with a video or coins, and daily reward claims (day number and coins).
  • Social: sharing an invite, opening an invite link (whether it came from a link or after install), and rematches. Never the room code.
  • Starting and finishing the tutorial.

Collected automatically by Firebase

An app-instance ID (a random identifier for this installation), device model, Android version, app version, language, approximate country or region derived from your IP address, and session timing.

App settings

The app also downloads a few settings from Firebase Remote Config (for example, whether online play is available). This uses the same random Firebase installation ID and sends no personal information.

Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a crash report so we can fix it. A report contains the technical details of the error (a stack trace), device model, Android version, app version, free memory and disk space, the random Firebase installation ID, and which screen, game and mode you were in (for example "Snakes & Ladders, vs computer"). It never contains player names, room codes, chat or anything you type.

Never collected

Player names or display names, room codes, player IDs, your contacts, your precise location, or anything you type. Our analytics events never include the advertising ID.

5. Advertising

Ludo Buddy is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. Ads only appear in these places:

  • Banner ads at the bottom of the menu screens: Home, player setup ("Who's playing?"), Play Online, the online waiting room, and Your stats.
  • Full-screen ads after a finished match (against the computer, pass & play or online), when you leave the results screen to go back to the menu or start a rematch. They are limited in frequency, so quick back-to-back matches don't each show one. Never during a match, and never when you quit a match early.
  • Rewarded videos, only when you choose to watch one: to unlock a board style, or to double your daily reward coins. They never play on their own. Rewards are cosmetic or in-game coins, which have no money value and can't be bought or cashed out.

Ads are never shown during play: the board, dice, moves, online matches and the results screen are always ad-free. Ads are only requested after the consent step described in section 6.

If you buy Remove ads, no ads of any kind are loaded or shown, including banners and full-screen ads, and every board is unlocked, so there are no videos to watch either.

To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (and an approximate location derived from it), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent and settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads on some devices).

Purchases

The optional one-time Remove ads purchase is sold and processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only receives the purchase status from Google Play (whether you own it) and remembers it on your phone, so it stays ad-free even offline. Firebase Analytics may automatically record the purchase (product, price and currency) for our sales reports. Google's handling of payment data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Your consent choices

If you're in a region where consent is required (such as the EEA, the UK or Switzerland), we show Google's consent form the first time you open the app. Until you answer, analytics and ad storage stay switched off. If you decline, ads are not requested and analytics stays limited to the basic, cookieless signals described in Google's consent mode.

You can change your choice at any time in Home → Privacy → Ad & analytics choices.

8. Sharing

Data is processed by the service providers named above, Google Firebase, Google AdMob and Google Play, to provide the features described here, or disclosed when required by law. Google processes Firebase data on our behalf under the Firebase data processing terms; see also Privacy and security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.

9. How long we keep data

  • Data on your phone: until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.
  • Online rooms, matches and emotes: deleted automatically 24 hours after the room is created.
  • Reports: kept up to 12 months for safety reviews, then deleted automatically.
  • Analytics events: kept in Firebase for up to 2 months, then deleted automatically. Aggregated reports that can't identify an installation may be kept longer.
  • Crash reports: kept in Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Your anonymous player ID: kept while the app is installed. Uninstalling removes it from your phone, and on request we delete it from Firebase.
  • Emails you send us (including deletion requests): as long as needed to answer them, and no longer than 12 months.

10. Your rights and choices

  • Change consent: Home → Privacy → Ad & analytics choices (where consent applies).
  • Delete local data: Android Settings → Apps → Ludo Buddy → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall.
  • Delete collected data: use the data deletion page. In the app, Home → Privacy → Copy my IDs copies the IDs we need to find your data. We delete it within 30 days.
  • Access, correction, objection: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have further rights, including to complain to your local data-protection authority. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

11. Children

Ludo Buddy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used online play and want their data deleted, use the deletion page or email us.

12. Security

Data sent to Firebase and Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Game state can only be changed by our server code, not by other players' apps. Data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox.

13. International transfers

Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under its standard contractual safeguards.

14. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the app's release notes.
