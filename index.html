Questions

Is Ludo Buddy free?

Yes. The whole game is free. It's supported by ads: banners on the menus and a short ad after a finished match, never during a turn. Three boards are free from the start; the others unlock forever with one short optional video each, and you can watch one to double your daily coins. Or buy "Remove ads" once to go ad-free and unlock every board.

Can I play without internet?

Yes. Vs Computer and Pass & Play work fully offline for both Ludo and Snakes & Ladders. Online play needs a connection.

How do I play with my friends?

Open Online Match, pick a game, then Create Room and share the 4-letter code. Friends enter it under "Join with a code". Sitting together? Use Pass & Play on one phone.

Are the online dice fair?

Yes. Online, every roll and move is made and checked on our server, not on anyone's phone, so they can't be faked.

Do I need an account?

No. Just type a nickname. Online play uses a random, anonymous player ID.

Is chat safe?

There is no free-text chat, only friendly predefined emotes. Tap any player's card to mute or report them.

What data do you collect?

Very little, and never your real name, contacts or precise location. Online matches are deleted after 24 hours. See the privacy policy for details, or ask us to delete your data.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 7.0 or newer.
