Delete my data

Ludo Buddy doesn't use accounts, so there's no account to close. This page explains how to delete what's on your phone and how to ask us to delete the little we hold. You don't need the app installed to make a request.

1. Data on your phone

Your board style, sound setting and tutorial progress are stored only on your device. To delete them:

  • Android Settings → Apps → Ludo Buddy → Storage → Clear storage, or
  • uninstall Ludo Buddy.

We can't see or delete this data for you, because it never reaches us.

2. Data we hold

  • Online matches (display name, moves, emotes): deleted automatically 24 hours after each room is created, so there's usually nothing left to delete.
  • Your anonymous player ID (if you played online) and any reports you made or that were made about you (kept up to 12 months).
  • Analytics linked to a random ID for your installation, deleted automatically after 2 months.

None of this is linked to your name, email or Google account, so we need your IDs to find it:

  1. Open Ludo Buddy.
  2. On the home screen, tap Privacy.
  3. Tap Copy my IDs and paste them below.

Already uninstalled? Send the request anyway. Without IDs we can't find analytics or player records, but they expire on their own as described above, and we'll delete any emails we hold from you.

Ad data is handled by Google. To reset or delete your advertising ID, open Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. To manage data Google holds about you, visit your Google Account.

3. Request deletion

What should we delete?

We confirm by email and delete within 30 days. We use your email only to answer this request, and delete it afterwards.

4. Questions

Email lunarsprintapps@gmail.com or read the privacy policy.
